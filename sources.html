Sources & provenance

Every load-bearing default on the calculator carries one of three labels and resolves to an entry on this page. External links are citations for you to follow — the site itself loads nothing from the network.

The [S] / [D] / [A] discipline

S  Stated

The value appears in a citable public source — an NVIDIA reference architecture, a vendor datasheet, a standard, a paper, a filing. The entry below names it and the access date.

D  Derived

Computed from stated values with the arithmetic shown — in the derivation chain under each headline number and in the per-row formula text.

A  Assumed

A planning assumption you should treat as an override-me default: engineering bands, project-specific unknowns, or values that genuinely have no public benchmark (those say so explicitly). Chips that link here mark inputs that are yours to set — target GPU counts, your tariff, your site climate.

Failure economics (spares, contingency, availability)

Spares/DOA replacement float — 2.5% of IT capex [D]

DOA ~10% of delivered systems + ~25% PCIe reseats at a documented 4k-GPU bring-up (Imbue) are vendor-RMA-covered — the owned cost is the FLOAT that rides the RMA pipeline, not a 10% capex multiplier. Sizing: 2.34 failures/1,000 node-days steady floor (Meta, arXiv:2410.21680) x ~25% needing physical swap [A] x 6-week RMA turnaround [A] = 2.46% of fleet in transit at any instant -> 2.5% default, band 1-4.5%. The float re-enters service and amortises with the fleet.

Method & scope

What this calculator is — and is not

A generic, client-side engineering estimator. It contains no project data, no customer data and no site data: defaults come from the public sources above, and everything project-specific is a user input. Results are planning-grade; they are not an offer, a quotation, or a design of record, and must be verified by licensed engineers, vendor submittals and your utility before any commitment.

The TCO model (T1–T8)

The TCO calculator is one levelization identity plus a monthly cash ledger:

T1 fleet     racks = ⌈gpus ÷ gpus_per_rack⌉ · it_kw = racks × rack_kw
T2 hours     gpu_hours/mo = gpus × 730 × utilization
T3 energy    LF = util + (1−util) × idle_frac · kWh/mo = it_kw × PUE × 730 × LF
T4 GPU value straight-line to salvage, or resale (1−d)^(m/12) with a floor
T5 facility   BUILD amortises $/W-IT over facility life; LEASE pays $/kW-month
T6 levelized [C0 − TV·df_N + Σ cash_m·df_m] ÷ Σ hours_m·df_m, df_m = (1+i)^−m
T7 cumulative cash out the door: C0 + running costs, NO terminal credit
T8 break-even first month where market_rate × delivered hours ≥ T7

T6 and T7 read differently by design: the levelized number consumes capital (residuals credited back), the cumulative curve is cash out the door.
