Turning the corpus into an operating discipline

A list of failure modes only pays for itself when it becomes procedure. The approach this corpus is built for has two halves. A field guide: every mode mapped to the checklist, hold point or test that catches it — receiving inspection with shock indicators, inspect-before-connect on every optical mate, torque witness marks plus IR scans during the load-bank window, full-load battery discharge before acceptance, integrated systems tests that deliberately create the failure (pull the plug, stop the duty pump, kill a UPS module) rather than wait for it. And an evidence model: every test leaves a time-stamped, per-asset artifact — certification records, settings baselines, event-recorder captures, discharge curves — so acceptance is a data package, not a walkthrough, and the first real incident is debugged from records instead of memory. The per-mode Detection and Prevention columns above are the raw material for both.
