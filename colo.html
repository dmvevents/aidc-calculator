Colo developer — build the facility, lease the kW

The landlord's side of the build-vs-lease trade: put up the shell and MEP (no GPUs — the published 10–13 $/W-IT facility band, the same basis as the TCO calculator's BUILD mode), lease capacity against the CBRE asking-rate anchors, and read the landlord cost floor $/kW-month, NOI, yield-on-cost, break-even occupancy and simple payback — then, with a loan-to-cost and a rate from your term sheet, the financing view: DSCR, debt yield and cash-on-cash. Tenant energy is metered pass-through — excluded on both sides. An estimator, not an underwriting: no leverage, no TI, no tax, and the notes say exactly what is out of scope. The GPU-owner side of this same trade is the TCO calculator.
